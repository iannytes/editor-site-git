Privacy Policy for Whispr

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

Introduction

Whispr ("the App") is developed by Ian Nytes.

Your privacy matters. Whispr is designed with a local-first philosophy, meaning your documents, notes, and reading data remain on your device whenever possible.

Information We Collect

Whispr does not require an account.

We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Payment information

Documents You Import

Whispr allows you to import files including:

  • PDF
  • EPUB
  • DOCX
  • Plain text
  • Other supported document formats

These files are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly export or share them using your device's built-in sharing features.

We do not upload your documents to our servers.

Notes and Voice Recordings

If you create:

  • Text notes
  • Voice notes
  • Reading annotations

they are stored locally on your device.

Voice recordings are processed locally whenever supported by your device.

Text-to-Speech

Whispr uses Android's built-in text-to-speech services.

Depending on the voice engine you choose (such as Google Text-to-Speech or another provider), portions of text may be processed according to that provider's privacy policy.

Whispr does not control or receive any information processed by third-party speech engines.

Permissions

Whispr may request permission to:

  • Read files you choose to import
  • Save exported documents
  • Record audio for voice notes (if enabled)

These permissions are used solely to provide requested features.

Internet Access

Most features work without an internet connection.

If future versions include optional online features (such as downloading voices, syncing data, AI-powered tools, or cloud backup), those features will clearly indicate when information is transmitted.

Third-Party Services

Whispr may rely on Android system services or third-party libraries to provide functionality.

Examples may include:

  • Android Text-to-Speech
  • Google Play Services (if installed on your device)

These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Because your information is stored primarily on your own device, you remain in control of your data.

However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Whispr is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any updates will be reflected by changing the Effective Date above.
